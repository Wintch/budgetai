# Quick-entry income and the hand-off to the job search (general)

Written 2026-10-20. General only, nothing about a person. Prices and rates come from news and agency pages found by search; **the official sites were not read** and nothing was tested.
Dated examples, not promises: check the official page before paying anything.

## 1. Scope: what this repo does and what it does not

- **This repo organises expenses** and points to cheap, fast ways to get some income while the crunch lasts.
- **The full job search lives in another repo (jobfinder):** the complete CV, profile and applications go there. Do not copy a CV here: duplicating it creates two versions that drift apart and
  puts personal data in a second place. Here we only advise "give your full CV to the job-search system".
- Rule of thumb: here, one line about what unlocks quick income; there, the long search for the best job.

## 2. What a person can be asked (profile for advice; a future assistant can ask these)

Age, sex, city or province, whether they hold a driving licence (and its class), what vehicle they have (bike, motorbike, car), household size, rough monthly income, due dates and amounts,
and whether they already filed their tax status. **Do not ask for identity numbers, bank details or passwords.** Age and sex are asked only because some steps depend on them
(minimum ages for professional licences, some programs). Answers stay on the user's side; nothing of this goes into a public repo.

## 3. The licence lever: a driving licence opens quick work

A licence is often the cheapest key to quick-entry work (delivery, messaging and courier services, driving jobs). Waiting usually costs more than getting it.

| item | amount (ARS) | source, date | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common licence from scratch, City of Buenos Aires | about 43,040 (issuing 34,200 + traffic-record certificate 8,840) | news, March 2026 | official rates change; check the City site |
| Common licence from scratch, a province (San Juan example) | about 71,840 | news, Feb 2026 | each jurisdiction sets its own fees |
| Professional licence (classes C, D, E) in the province of Buenos Aires, new scheme | up to about 250,000 including mandatory evaluations and courses | news, 2026 | does **not** apply to the common A and B classes |
| Old aggregator figure (5,000 to 15,000) | outdated | agency aggregator | ignore |

- Steps in the usual order: the traffic-record certificate (CENAT), a medical/psychophysical check, a road-safety course with a theory exam, then a practical exam; book the appointment on the official platform.
- **An unpaid traffic fine can block a licence renewal** (secondary source): check the plate and the holder's record before paying fees (`TRAFFIC_FINES.md`).
- Which class a motorbike courier needs (usually A) and the waiting time for an appointment were **not found**: ask the issuing office.

## 4. Delivery and courier work: what the numbers say (read before choosing it)

- Average income per order, March 2026: about 3,165 ARS, before tips and **before the worker's own costs** (fuel, maintenance, insurance, phone) (news, from a published index of two large apps).
  Another note says one app raised its pay to about 3,924 per trip while the other froze it.
- Orders needed per month (same source): about 111 for the minimum wage, about 453 for the household basic basket, about 13 for one monotributo quota.
- Rough payback of a licence (calc, gross, before costs): 43,040 / 3,165 = **about 14 orders**; 250,000 / 3,165 = about 79 orders. Net of costs it is more; the point is that the licence pays back in days of work, not months, if the work exists.
- Not confirmed: sign-up requirements per platform (tax status, licence, insurance, vehicle), which apps accept a car, and the platform-specific rules for riders (a court ruling asking for helmet, anchored box and accident insurance was found, undated: **do not treat it as current**).
- Risks: accidents, no employer benefits, costs that eat the gross, and fines. Compare with other quick work before committing.

## 4b. Trades and small repairs: a free lead channel (not verified)

A person with a trade (plumbing, electrical, painting, cleaning, appliance repair) can look for leads on an online home-services marketplace such as servidos.ar (`PORTALS.md`): the site says a profile and leads are free and it takes no commission, but that is its own claim, read on one day, and its terms were not read here. Cheaper than the delivery route when the person already has the skill and tools.
The other direction is as useful: a person who **needs** a repair posts the job for free and compares quotes before paying anyone. Always get two or three quotes, split labour and materials, and agree the scope in writing.
Not verified: how many jobs are posted in a given area, the real response time, how the paid "Pro" account changes the order of leads, and whether the work counts toward the monotributo category (it does if invoiced; ask the accountant).

## 5. Checklist for the person

1. Do you have a valid licence of the right class? If not, price it (section 3) and compare with the orders or hours needed to recover it.
2. Is a pending fine blocking it? Look at the plate first.
3. Pick the vehicle you already own; do not buy one for this.
4. Register the tax status that the platform requires (verify on the platform and the tax agency).
5. Give the complete CV to the job-search system (other repo) for the longer search.
