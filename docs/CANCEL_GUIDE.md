# Cancelling a paid AI subscription (Claude Pro) without paying one more cycle

Written 2026-10-19 from a real cancellation. Part of the cost ladder: a fixed monthly fee is the first thing to cut when the free
tiers and the home model cover the work (see `COST_LADDER.md`).

What was observed (Claude Pro, billed monthly, web account) is marked **seen**. What is general knowledge and was not checked here
is marked **not verified**.

## 1. Before pressing anything

1. Find the renewal date. **Seen:** Settings > Billing shows "Your subscription will auto renew on <date>". Cancel before that day.
2. Check how you pay. **Not verified:** a plan bought through an app store (Apple, Google Play) is cancelled in that store, not on the
   website.
3. Export what you want to keep. **Seen:** Settings > Privacy has "Export data". Cancelling stops billing; it does not delete the account.
4. Check what hangs from the account: API keys, integrations, a router or script that authenticates with it. Move them first.
5. Check the invoice list in Billing. Seeing what was actually charged each month is often the real reason to cancel.

## 2. Cancel

1. Settings > **Billing** (not Account: Account only tells you to cancel the plan before deleting the account).
2. At the bottom, **Cancel plan**, then confirm.
3. A survey appears ("What is your main reason for canceling?"). **Seen:** it is optional; the cancellation does not depend on answering.
   Free-text boxes are optional too. Do not put personal or financial details in them: the text goes to the vendor.

## 3. Verify it took effect

Reload Billing. **Seen**, the signs of a firm cancellation:

- The plan line changes from "will auto renew on <date>" to "Your subscription will be canceled on <date>".
- The word "auto renew" is gone.
- The invoice list has no new charge on the next date.

If the page still says "auto renew", the cancellation did not register: repeat step 2.

## 4. After

- Access normally continues until the date shown. Use it to finish whatever depends on the paid plan.
- A week after the date, look at the card statement: there should be no charge. Check again the following month.
- Record it in your own ledger: plan, amount, date cancelled, date access ends, monthly saving.
- Account deletion is a separate step, available once the plan is cancelled. Do it only after the export.

## Checklist

- [ ] Renewal date known
- [ ] Payment channel known (web vs app store)
- [ ] Data exported
- [ ] Keys and integrations moved
- [ ] Cancel plan pressed
- [ ] Billing page shows "will be canceled on <date>", no "auto renew"
- [ ] Ledger updated
- [ ] No charge on the next statement
