# Convenio Multilateral returns (general)

If a monotributista is registered in provincial gross-income tax **under the Convenio Multilateral** (typical when clients sit in other provinces), the picture "one payment a month and no returns" is wrong.

## 1. What is due

| duty | form | how often | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly return and payment of the provincial gross-income tax, split between jurisdictions | CM03 | monthly (the due date is set each year by the Comision Arbitral; mid-month, by the last digit of the tax id; see the table below) | "Convenio Multilateral - SIFERE Web - DDJJ" service |
| Annual return | CM05 | yearly; the one for fiscal year 2025 is reported due **2026-06-30**, extended in some past years | same service, "annual returns" tab |
| Registration of jurisdictions | registry | once, then on changes | registration certificate (provincial) lists them |

CM03 due dates for the 2026 instalments, by last digit of the tax id (RG (CA) 20/2025, read on 2026-11-18 from a secondary copy; the official annex is only in the Boletin Oficial web edition and was **not read**; a weekend or holiday moves a date to the next business day):

| instalment | 0 to 2 | 3 to 5 | 6 to 7 | 8 to 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Jan | 02-13 | 02-18 | 02-19 | 02-20 |
| 02 Feb | 03-13 | 03-16 | 03-17 | 03-18 |
| 03 Mar | 04-15 | 04-16 | 04-17 | 04-20 |

(Dates are 2026 month-day.) **The October instalment was rescheduled** because of the extraordinary holidays of 9 to 11 November 2026: Presidency Disposition 11/2026 (Boletin Oficial 2026-10-19), ratified by RG (CA) 11/2026 (BO 2026-10-22), moved the SIFERE return **and payment** to **2026-11-17 (digits 0 to 2), 11-18 (3 to 5), 11-19 (6 to 7), 11-20 (8 to 9)**. Read from a secondary source on 2026-11-18; the official text was not read. Lesson: a due-date table goes stale; check the agency's "vencimientos" page and recent dispositions every month before calling a filing late. Cross-check: nine real presentations of one filer (digit 6 to 7) fell on the due date or 1 to 3 days before it, every month from the January to the September instalment, which supports the table. Look for extensions (SIFERE outages have produced them). The page read did not mention a late-filing fine; the amount is not verified.

The provincial tax is **not** part of the monotributo quota in that case: the unified quota shows the simplified provincial tax as "not applicable by Convenio Multilateral".

## 2. How to check your own standing (read-only)

1. Registration certificate: "IIBB Convenio Multilateral - active", with the jurisdictions and the start month.
2. SIFERE Web, monthly list: one row per month since the start, each "presented" with a transaction number. Presentation days seen: between the 10th and the 19th of the following month.
3. SIFERE Web, annual list: an empty list for a past fiscal year means nothing was presented.
4. SIFERE Web, payment slips: unpaid vouchers.

## 3. Reading a monthly draft

A draft shows, per jurisdiction: determined tax, credits that subtract (withholdings, previous balance), credits that add, balance in your favour and amount to pay. One observed draft: income of 150,000 in a month with a determined tax of 5,250 in the seat jurisdiction, i.e. 3.5% (rate of that activity and period **not verified**), and zero in the other jurisdiction because the month's invoice belonged to a local client. A month with a credit larger than the tax gives "to pay 0" but must still be filed.

## 3b. Late filing and fines

- Fines for a late or missing return are applied **by each local jurisdiction**, not by the Comision Arbitral. In Buenos Aires province the formal-duty fine for not filing on time comes from article 60 (sixth paragraph) of the Fiscal Code, is automatic without prior notice, and is reported in the electronic tax domicile and the fines system (secondary sources). Amounts seen are **old** (2016: a few hundred pesos for an individual, halved if paid within 15 days of the notice); the current amount was **not found**. Pay it under the right concept (a wrong concept led to a later demand in a forum case).
- Interest runs only on **unpaid tax**; a zero return owes none (general rule, not verified for each province).
- The Comision Arbitral has several times extended deadlines or declared filings on time when SIFERE failed (anticipo 1/2026 until 2026-02-20; anticipo 10/2025 until 2025-11-20), so a filing a day or two late may be on time by disposition.

## 3c. Article 14 (start of activity) and the annual return

- Secondary sources: to use the coefficient of a year, the activity must have run **more than three months (about 90 days) before 31 December** of the base year; otherwise **100% of income is assigned to the jurisdiction that originates it** and the normal split starts the following year. That matches coefficient 0.0000 with "Articulo 14" on screen.
- A paywalled consultation is titled "obligation to file the CM05 without a coefficient for activity under 90 days": a start in December probably still files the CM05 of that year with no coefficient. **Not verified**; the official instructions and the Comision Arbitral were not read. Ask them before assuming it was or was not due.

## 4. Filing a monthly return by hand (SIFERE Web)

Observed on 2026-11-18 by a user filing a late zero-income month, screen by screen. These are the labels seen on screen, not an official manual, and a screen can change. The sworn statement is always made by the taxpayer, never by a script.

1. **List.** "Declaraciones Juradas Mensuales" lists one row per instalment with a state: `1 - BORRADOR`, `2 - CERRADA`, `4 - PRESENTADA` (with the presentation date and "OK Transaccion: <number>"). A month with no row has not been created.
2. **Create.** "Nueva Declaracion Jurada Mensual": choose year and month, then **"Crear DJ desde"** (copy of the last presented one: same coefficients, rates and splits, all data kept) or a fresh one built from the registry (Padron Web). Copy unless the registered activities or jurisdictions changed.
3. **Walk the tree** on the left ("Items de la DDJJ"). The copy does not necessarily carry the old amounts, but check every node:
   - *Datos a Nivel Pais*: jurisdictions (coefficient 0.0000 with "Articulo 14: SI" is the start-of-activity regime, "Inicio Actividades (Articulo 14)" on the billing screen; how long it lasts is not verified), activities, billing (gravados / no gravados), deductions.
   - *Per jurisdiction*: activities (taxable base, rate, instalment tax), other debits, other credits, non-bank payments, **previous balances in favour** (the system loads the last presented return's balance by itself; do not add it again with the manual form), instalment credits.
   - *Liquidacion Final*: **signer data** and "Finalizar DDJJ".
4. **Signer data is copied from the last return.** Check the e-mail (it may belong to whoever filed before, for example an accountant), and that surname and first name are in their own fields. Save with "Actualizar"; do not press "Traer datos de ultima DJ presentada" afterwards, it reloads the old values.
5. **Deductions (withholdings, bank collections, perceptions).** The system loads the ones registered by agents for the period. They appear in "Carga de Deducciones" (the detail shows date, agent, amount, state) but are **not applied to the return until you press the green "Incluir deducciones en DJ"** button. Afterwards "Datos de Deducciones" shows them and the final totals change. A perception is a credit: it lowers the tax, and with a zero tax it adds to the balance in favour. Do not delete or upload records unless you know what they are.
6. **Final totals** ("Totales por Jurisdiccion"): base, instalment tax, values that add, values that subtract, in favour of the tax agency, in favour of the taxpayer, total to pay. Check them against your own calculation before closing.
7. **Close.** "Finalizar DDJJ" asks for confirmation ("all validations passed, you can CLOSE the return to present it") and sets the state to `2 - CERRADA`. This is **not** the presentation.
8. **Present.** In the row's "Acciones" menu: **"Presentar DJ ante ARCA"**. A notice first says the presentation is "EN PROCESO" (a few minutes); then the state becomes `4 - PRESENTADA` with "OK Transaccion: <number>". A zero-payment return needs no payment slip. Save the number and the PDF. If the total to pay is not zero, a payment slip is needed ("Volantes de Pagos"): not observed yet.

Order that worked: create (copy) -> signer data -> activities per jurisdiction -> previous balances -> deductions and "Incluir deducciones en DJ" -> final totals -> close -> present.

## 5. Automation status

No API and no open-source filer found for SIFERE Web (2026-11-02, US-only search, so absence is not proof). A commercial helper builds import spreadsheets for the annual return. Sensible routes: remind monthly (`tools/arca/due_calendar.py` adds a reminder on the 10th), compute the draft locally from the invoice list, file by hand.

## 6. Not verified

See the front-matter. Ask the accountant or the Comision Arbitral for the exact 2026 calendar by tax-id ending, and whether a registration made in December owes the annual return for that year.
