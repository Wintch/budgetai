# Fan support and tipping platforms for artists and creators: Cafecito and alternatives

Where a fan can send a small sum to an artist, what each platform takes, and how the money reaches an Argentine account. Method and comparison only: no one's handle belongs here. Companion to `FUNDING_LINKS.md` (how a funding link attaches to a repository).

## Cafecito (Argentina)
- **What it is:** an Argentine page where fans send a "cafecito" (a tip, default 50 ARS in press accounts, creator-adjustable) or buy a plan or exclusive content. Press describes it as the most used platform of this kind in the country (Cronista, La Nacion, 2026-11-03, not dated on the page).
- **Setup:** profile creation "takes 60 seconds" (home page). Age 18 or more is required to create an account, publish or receive payments; some features need identity verification, extra data or an active payout method (FAQ requirements page; no date on it).
- **Payout methods:** set from the Pagos section: Mercado Pago can be connected; bank transfer and crypto are named as other options; requirements depend on the method (FAQ payout-setup page). Payments from fans can be processed by Mercado Pago, Galio Pay (bank transfer) or Amplify (crypto), per the terms of 2026-08-25.
- **Fees:** the official FAQ and terms say Cafecito's commission and the payment method's cost "are shown on the platform and at checkout" and give no number. Press (Cronista; a search snippet of an older FAQ) says **5% per transaction, no monthly fee, deducted before the transfer**. **Treat 5% as unverified until read at checkout or in the creator's Pagos screen.** The payment method's own cost (for example Mercado Pago) is separate and may stack.
- **Withdrawal:** only bank transfer is described on the FAQ page, possibly with identity and bank-detail checks; no minimum, timing or fee is stated there. The Pagos screen of an account is where to read them.
- **Taxes:** the terms say the creator must meet their own tax obligations ("cumplir con las obligaciones fiscales que le correspondan"). Whether received tips count as monotributo income is not settled here: ask an accountant (see `ARCA_RULES_MAP.md` for the caps).
- **Content rules:** adult creators need identity and age verification; illegal, rights-infringing or deceptive material is prohibited (terms).

## What to weigh (order of cheapness)
1. **Total take, not the headline.** Platform commission plus the processor plus conversion plus the cost of moving the money out. A 0% platform can still cost 3-6% in processing.
2. **Currency.** Pesos from Argentine fans stay in pesos (Cafecito, Mercado Pago link). Dollars from abroad need a route that pays out to Argentina; Argentine press reports that PayPal converts to pesos and charges per withdrawal (iProfesional, Cronista, dates not checked).
3. **Recurring vs one-off.** Tips are small and irregular (see the donations note in `FUNDING_LINKS.md`); memberships need content on a schedule.
4. **Identity.** Every platform links a public profile to the person; verification needs real documents.
5. **Taxes.** Received money may count as income; check with an accountant before relying on it.
6. **Lock-in.** Keep the audience list or a link of your own, so changing platform does not lose it.
