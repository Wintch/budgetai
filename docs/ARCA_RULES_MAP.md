# ARCA rules map for a monotributista (general)

Written 2026-11-03 from a research round of three searches (law and recategorisation; fines and relief; invoicing and account hygiene). **Most items were read through a summarising fetcher or in press; several official pages did not load.** Each claim is tagged: **[official]** = the norm or an ARCA/Argentina.gob.ar/Infoleg/Boletin Oficial page was read; **[press]** = accountancy or news sites; **[unverified]** = could not be confirmed. Not tax advice: it says what the rules reportedly are, where to confirm them, and what to ask. Amounts change; the dates below are the point.

## 1. The legal frame in force (as read)

- The regime is **Ley 24.977 (Annex)** as amended by **Ley 27.743** (2024), Title VI arts. 85-97 [official]: income ceiling = category K; maximum unit price of goods; categories A-K; the integrated tax with a possible bonus of up to 20%; the exclusion causes; one-time excess allowance; **art. 94: amounts are updated every six months from 2025, in January and July**; art. 96: people who left the regime may re-join once.
- Regulation: **RG 4309/2018** (the working rules), amended by **RG 5546/2024** (dates, expense thresholds, rentals) and **RG 5637/2025** (ex officio recategorisation; read in press only) [official / press]. Automatic removal for debt is based on Decreto 1/2010 art. 36 (cited through RG 4309 art. 66; the decree text itself was not read) [official, indirect].
- 2026: no new law on the regime itself was found, only resolutions and the automatic updates. Press reports **RG 5893/2026** making electronic invoicing mandatory for social and promoted monotributistas from 2026-11-01 [press, not verified]. Not relevant to an ordinary monotributista.
- General penalty and procedure law changed in 2026: **Ley 27.799** ("Inocencia Fiscal", Boletin Oficial 2026-01-02) and **Ley 27.826** (BO 2026-10-09, modifies Ley 27.799 and Ley 11.683) [official]. Section 5.

## 2. Recategorisation

| item | rule as read |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | Two a year, for the semesters ending in June and December. RG 4309 art. 20 (RG 5546): done **by the 5th of August and of February** (next business day if the 5th is not one). Reading: the window opens in January and July and **closes on 5 Feb and 5 Aug** [official]. 2026 deadlines were 5 Feb and 5 Aug [press]. **2027 is not announced**: by the norm, the window closes on Friday 5 Feb 2027 [unverified] |
| **What is compared** | The last 12 months of gross income and electricity consumed, plus surface and rent accrued, at the end of each semester (art. 19) [official] |
| **If you do nothing** | The previous category is ratified (art. 21) [official] |
| **Unit price of goods** | A requirement of the regime and an exclusion cause, not a recategorisation parameter [official] |
| **New category applies from** | The August accrual (June semester) or the February accrual (December semester) [official] |
| **Scale table** | Published by ARCA with a validity date ("valores desde el 1/08/2026" when read); updated twice a year in January and July, effective 1 Feb and 1 Aug. The index used is not named in the text read; press says CPI-based [official / press]. The Feb 2026 increase was 14.29% [press] |

### Activity of less than 12 months (the uncertain rule)
Read on 2026-11-03 from the consolidated RG 4309 (Infoleg) and the Annex of Ley 24.977 (AFIP library, text labelled "vigente segun Ley 27.430"; **the wording after Ley 27.743 was not shown on that page**, so check it) [official]:

- **Annex art. 12, paragraph 1 (start of activity):** you are placed in the category that matches your surface and rent, or a "reasonable estimate" if you have no such references.
- **Annex art. 12, paragraph 2 (the one early review):** "transcurridos seis (6) meses" you must **annualise** the gross income, electricity consumed and rents accrued in that period, "a efectos de confirmar su categorizacion o determinar su recategorizacion o exclusion". Any change applies **from the second month after the last month of that six-month period**. The text read does not give the arithmetic of the annualisation (the usual reading is income over the months, times 12) and does not say what happens if the annualised figure passes the top cap beyond naming exclusion as a possible result.
- **RG 4309 art. 21 b):** a taxpayer who is starting is **exempt from the semester recategorisation** (art. 20) "por el periodo comprendido entre el mes de inicio hasta que no haya transcurrido un semestre calendario completo", and meanwhile pays the amount from the start-of-activity procedure of Annex art. 12. Art. 21's last paragraph: not recategorising "implicara la ratificacion de la categoria declarada con anterioridad".
- **RG 4309 art. 11:** replacing the declared activity by one of a different group counts as a new start of activity; adding an activity or replacing one inside the same group does not.
- **"You can recategorise only once in the first year" was not found** in any of these texts. What the texts give is: one **annualisation review after 6 months** (Annex art. 12), an **exemption from the semester recategorisation until one full calendar semester has passed** (art. 21 b), and then the **ordinary semester windows** (5 Feb and 5 Aug) with the 12-month sum (art. 19). Press repeats the 6-month review and the annualisation; no source read says that a second change in the first year is forbidden [unverified].
- Worked timeline, for a start in December of year Y (calc, reading of the texts, not confirmed): the December semester is not a full calendar semester, so there is no February duty in year Y+1 (exempt); the January-June semester of year Y+1 is a full one, so its recategorisation falls due **by 5 August of year Y+1**; the Annex art. 12 six-month review lands around June-July of year Y+1. Both reviews then point to the same period, which is why press and accountants talk about "August" for a December start.
- ARCA's help page says people with "less than 6 months" need not act [official], consistent with art. 12 (the first review comes at 6 months). Press says that from 6 to under 12 months income is annualised [press]; that matches art. 12 paragraph 2.

### Ex officio recategorisation by ARCA
- Grounds (RG 4309 arts. 22-23, RG 5637): you did not recategorise, or did it inaccurately; or purchases, expenses, assets or bank credits exceed your current cap (new category set on those amounts plus 20% or 30% by activity type); beyond the exclusion thresholds it becomes exclusion [official].
- ARCA notifies in the **Domicilio Fiscal Electronico** within 10 business days after the deadline; the systemic-control window in 2026 was reported as 6-23 Feb [press]. For the June semester the new category applies from the August accrual to January; for the December semester from the February accrual to July [official].
- You can dispute ("disconformidad") through "Presentaciones Digitales" within **15 days of the notice** [official].
- **Best practice**: recategorise yourself before ARCA notifies anything (section 5 shows why).

## 3. Exclusion and removal

- **Exclusion causes** (ARCA page, Ley 24.977 art. 20) [official]: income above the top cap; rent or surface above the cap; unit price above the maximum; purchases, expenses and bank deposits incompatible with declared income; imports for resale; more than 3 simultaneous activities or units; goods registered for a services taxpayer; **operations not invoiced**; purchases plus costs reaching 80% (goods) or 40% (services, leases, works) of category K's cap; listing in REPSAL.
- Effect: you leave the regime and are registered in the general taxes from the date of exclusion (RG 4309 arts. 53, 61). Appeal: 15 days after a systemic control; after an on-site audit, a written response in 10 days and then 15 days [official]. Re-entry after exclusion or voluntary renunciation: **3 full calendar years**, with the one-time exception in Ley 27.743 art. 96 [official].
- **Removal for unpaid quota**: after **10 consecutive unpaid months** ARCA removes you automatically. To return you must pay the debt of those 10 months plus any formal sanctions (RG 4309 art. 66) [official]. The statute article for the 10 months was not found [unverified].
- What exclusion or removal does to invoices already issued, and to issuing new ones: **not covered by any source read**. Press says you lose the ability to invoice [unverified].
- **Voluntary baja**: portal "Modificacion y baja" then "Darse de baja"; the month must be paid and the baja takes effect the next month; pension and health contributions through the monotributo end then [official].
- **Pension and health insurance**: the quota includes both unless you are an employee (the employer provides the insurance), a retiree, or one of the listed exceptions. You choose the insurer from the health superintendency's list; changes once a calendar year with a 12-month minimum stay (Decreto 955/2024, press) [official / press]. **Activation requires completing the affiliation with the chosen insurer**; what happens if you never do is not stated on the official pages (press says one is assigned) [unverified].

## 4. Invoicing rules and good practice

- A monotributista issues type **C** (type **E** for exported services, on a separate point of sale). Needs fiscal key **level 3**. Credit notes C correct errors; an invoice is never deleted [official].
- **When to invoice a service** (RG 5866/2026, in force 2026-07-01, replacing art. 13 of RG 1415): on the day the service is concluded **or** the price is received, whichever comes first; continuous services at the end of each calendar month [official, Boletin Oficial].
- **CAE window**: ARCA's e-invoicing page says the CAE for a service may be requested up to **10 calendar days before or after** the date written on the invoice; the invoice must be available to the buyer within 10 days of the CAE [official]. Whether RG 5866 changed this was not confirmed. Plain rule: invoice when the work is done or paid, not weeks later. How far back a late invoice may be dated was not found in an official source.
- **Receiver's IVA condition**: mandatory on every electronic invoice since RG 5616/2024 [official]. A rejection of invoices missing it from 2026-12-01 is reported by press only [press]. A monotributista still issues C; the field says what the buyer is (final consumer, monotributista, etc.).
- **Final consumer identification** (RG 5866 annex): the legend "A CONSUMIDOR FINAL"; from **$10,000,000** the buyer's DNI/CUIL/CDI is required; below that name and address may be "NR"; if the buyer needs the invoice to deduct tax, the CUIT is required at any amount [official].
- **Work priced in dollars for a local client**:
  - Invoiced and paid in the same foreign currency: the buyer-side rule is the **Banco de la Nacion seller ("vendedor divisa") rate of the previous business day**, filled in by the electronic system (RG 5616 art. 1, press quoting the text) [press].
  - A peso invoice for dollar-priced work: no ARCA criterion found. Pick a documented rate (the official BCRA rate of the day is a defensible choice) and **write the rate and date in the invoice detail or a note kept with it** [unverified, practical].
- **Exports** (type E): pesos at the **BNA buyer rate of the previous day** [official]; the person must stay within the top-category cap; monotributistas with a MiPyME certificate are exempt from export duties [official].
- **Withholding by clients**: ARCA's FAQ (RG 2616/2009) says the buyer checks your registration certificate and **does not withhold while the accumulated operations over 12 months stay below the cap of your activity**; above it, withholding applies [official, as quoted by search; the page did not load]. Rates and current cap not confirmed. In the province of Buenos Aires the simplified gross-income regime is reported as outside withholdings [press]. Keep every certificate with the invoices.
- **Records**: the general limitation period is 5 years (Ley 11.683 art. 56). Ley 27.799 and IG 3/2026 reportedly cut it to 3 years for a **registered taxpayer who filed on time and paid**, and Ley 27.826 is reported to reduce it from 5 to 3 [press / official summary]. Whether a monotributista qualifies is disputed. **Keep invoices and payment proofs at least 5 years; 10 is the safe number** (retention rule itself not confirmed) [unverified].
- **Security**: never share, save in a browser, reuse or send the fiscal key by any channel; ARCA never asks for it by phone, email or WhatsApp; change it periodically [official]. To let an accountant act, **delegate the services in "Administrador de Relaciones"** (the delegate accepts with their own level-3 key) and revoke when it ends [official / press]. Two-step verification and a login-history view: nothing official found [unverified].

## 5. Fines, interest and what they cost

| situation | consequence as read | basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Category too low** | Fine of **50% of the integrated tax and pension contribution** for not recategorising or doing it inaccurately. ARCA recategorises ex officio. **Half off if you accept within 15 days of the notice; no fine if you recategorise correctly before ARCA notifies the debt.** | Ley 24.977 Annex art. 26 b) and c) [official] |
| **Not invoicing** | Clausura of 1 to 5 days for monotributistas; the general rule is 2 to 6 days (operation over $20,000) and doubles if repeated within 2 years. Also an exclusion cause with a 3-year wait | Annex art. 26 a); Ley 11.683 art. 40 as raised by Ley 27.799 arts. 28-29 [official] |
| **Quota not paid** | Resarcitorio interest from each due date, capped at twice the highest rate of the Banco de la Nacion; **2.75% a month from 2025-07-01**, later change not found. Not an art. 40 infraction. 10 months unpaid = removal | Ley 11.683 art. 37; Res. MECON 823/2025 (secondary); Decreto 1/2010 art. 36 [official / press] |
| **Return or information not filed** | $220,000 per omitted return, halved if filed and paid within 15 days of the notice; formal duties $150,000 to $2,500,000. Ley 27.826 adds an automatic 25% reduction for natural persons and MiPyMEs on these fines and a one-time 50% reduction of unpaid formal fines | Ley 11.683 arts. 38-39; Ley 27.799 art. 15; Ley 27.826 art. 49 bis [official] |
| **Evasion** | Criminal thresholds raised (simple evasion from 1.5 million to 100 million per tax and year); no criminal complaint if principal and interest are paid before it is filed (once per taxpayer) | Ley 27.799 [official] |

Amounts in Ley 11.683 are indexed by the UVA from 2027-01-01 [official]. Which conduct the $200,000 to $7,500,000 range of art. 40 covers was not clear [unverified].

### Domicilio Fiscal Electronico (DFE)
- Registering it is mandatory (RG 4280 art. 1). A notification posted there is valid and effective [official].
- It is **deemed delivered the day you open it; if you never open it, at 00:00 on the Monday after it was posted** (next business day if that is a holiday) (RG 4280 art. 7). The email or SMS is only an alert; you are expected to look regularly (arts. 5-6) [official].
- **The deadlines run even if you never open it.** Ex officio recategorisation, requirements and proceedings all arrive there.
- Answer times: an electronic requirement (SIACE) gives **15 business days** and one extension of up to 15 requested before the deadline; the "vista" before a determination gives 15 days, extendable once [official]. The consequences of not answering a requirement are not stated on the pages read.

### Payment plans and relief
- **RG 5875/2026** (BO 2026-07-14): a special plan for debts due up to 2026-06-30, through "Mis Facilidades": up to 18 instalments, 5% down payment, 2.75% monthly interest, $50,000 minimum per instalment; it does not forgive interest or fines [official]. Adhesion reportedly extended to 2026-12-30 by RG 5920/2026 [press]. Whether it covers monotributo debt is not confirmed; press says small taxpayers get the micro terms.
- **Permanent monotributo plan** (RG 4166), for taxpayers excluded from the regime: request within the third month after exclusion; 2% down payment within 30 days (otherwise 5%); up to 36 instalments; debit by registered bank account from 2026-10-01 [official].
- No amnesty or moratorium was identified.

## 6. How to pay the least (summary)

1. Pay every quota by its due date (direct debit); 10 missed months is the cliff.
2. **Recategorise yourself** in the January and July windows, before 5 Feb and 5 Aug; it removes the fine and keeps you in control of the month your new quota starts.
3. Keep a running total of invoices of the last 12 months against the cap (`tools/arca/category_calc.py`) and check before **each** invoice that is large.
4. Invoice on the day of the work or the payment; correct errors with a credit note, never by waiting.
5. Look at the DFE at least weekly (put it in the calendar).
6. Delegate instead of sharing the key; keep a password manager and change the key after any exposure.
7. If you are behind, ask for a plan before the debt reaches 10 months.
